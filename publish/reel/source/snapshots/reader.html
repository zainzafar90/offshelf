The quiet web

1 of 1 images offline

Small sites, long essays and the pleasure of reading one thing at a time.

For a long time we treated the web like a library that would always be open. Then pages moved, posts vanished, and the tab you meant to come back to quietly closed itself.

Keeping a copy used to mean printing it. Now it can mean one click: the words, the images, the thread in the order it was written, stored on your own device.

A small promise

A saved page is a small promise to your future self. It will be there when the network isn't, and it will look the way you like to read.

The best reading tools get out of the way. A clear title, a comfortable line length and a way back to your library are usually enough.

A quiet shelf

No account. No sync server. Just a quiet shelf that is yours, with everything you meant to come back to.

Some things deserve more than an open tab. Save them once, and read them when you have the space.

You’re all caught up.
